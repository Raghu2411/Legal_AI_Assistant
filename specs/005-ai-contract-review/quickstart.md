# Quickstart: AI Contract Review (Step 5)

## Prerequisites
- **Admin**: Ensure "Golden Rules" are populated in the Admin Console.
- **Lawyer**: Ensure a document has been successfully uploaded and vectorized (Step 4).

## Launching the Review Studio
1. Navigate to the **Client Detail** page for the target client.
2. Under the **Documents** tab, locate the document you wish to review.
3. Click the **Review Studio** icon (newly added for Step 5).
4. **Instant Scan (Principle XVII)**: The system will automatically start the AI scan. A loading spinner will indicate progress (<15s target).

## Using the Three-Pane Interface
- **Left Pane (Risk List)**: A list of AI-detected risks grouped by Traffic Light color (Red/Yellow/Green) and scan history.
- **Center Pane (Document)**: The interactive document editor (TipTap). View and edit the refined contract content.
- **Right Pane (Action Studio)**: Detailed AI reasoning and Manual Override controls.

## Reviewing AI Suggestions
1. Click **Review AI Suggestion** on a risk item in the Left Pane.
2. A side-by-side comparison modal opens.
3. Click **Accept & Replace** to update the document state immediately.

## Performing Manual Overrides
1. Select a risk in the Middle Pane.
2. In the Action Area, click **Override Status**.
3. Choose the new risk level and provide a mandatory **Rationale**.
4. The risk color will update to reflect your manual decision.

## Completing the Review
1. Once all risks are addressed, click **Mark as Reviewed** in the top-right header.
2. The document status will transition to **Reviewed**.
3. Use the **Export** button to download the final version as a PDF or DOCX.
